# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Nova Tables to be a welcoming and inclusive project for everyone, regardless of experience
level, background or identity. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for all.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and any
other channel where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Instances of
abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the maintainer
at [novaardiansyah78@gmail.com](mailto:novaardiansyah78@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.
